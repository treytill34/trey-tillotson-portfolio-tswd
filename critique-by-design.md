# Women and Nonbinary Music Directors on Broadway Shows


## Step one: the visualization

The original data was compiled by Sariva Goetz at https://maestramusic.org/resources/timelines/timeline-of-broadway-conductors-and-mds/. I found out about this source through the link https://makeovermonday.vercel.app/dataset/2026w3-women-conductors-on-broadway.

## Step two: the critique

The original design was engaging, especially as it was very interactive. However, I mostly wanted to simplify the data presented to help viewers see a story at a glance. 

I noticed that the graphic was focused on the broadway show instead of the person. However, after looking at the data, there were a lot of start dates for individuals missing, which made it difficult to orient the narrative toward the directors.

## Step three: Sketch a solution

In my solution, I opted to show each show as a circle for simplicity. I kept the original blue and orange color scheme, but added a key.

## Step four: Test the solution

When sharing my sketch with colleagues, they agreed with my approach with the given data. Ultimately, the redesign was helpful for them, but they mostly made small critiques to the key and chart for optimal clarification.

## Step five: build the solution

I initially attempted to use NotebookLM or Gemini (under an education account) to recreate my sketch. However, each AI tool was not successful. Gemini made the data into a new spreadsheet that was more easily read in Tableau. I used Tableau's AI chatbot, Piper, to assist me in creating the final solution.
